# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends.

This project was created as part of a Dashboard Design task to help business stakeholders make informed decisions through data visualization and analytics.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

* Analyze sales and profit performance.
* Monitor key business KPIs.
* Identify high-performing products and regions.
* Track sales trends over time.
* Enable interactive data exploration using filters and slicers.
* Support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Sample Superstore Dataset**
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence Reporting
* KPI Analysis

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains retail sales transaction records, including:

* Order Date
* Sales
* Profit
* Category
* Segment
* Region
* Product Name
* Customer Information

**Total Records:** ~9,994 Orders

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

* **Total Sales:** 2.30M
* **Total Profit:** 286.40K
* **Total Orders:** 9,994
* **Profit Margin:** 12.47%

### 🔹 Sales Trend Analysis

* Line Chart visualization
* Tracks sales performance over time
* Identifies growth patterns and business trends

### 🔹 Sales by Category

* Compares performance across:

  * Technology
  * Furniture
  * Office Supplies

### 🔹 Profit by Region

* Evaluates profitability across different regions
* Helps identify high-performing markets

### 🔹 Sales by Segment

* Analyzes contribution from:

  * Consumer
  * Corporate
  * Home Office

### 🔹 Top Products Analysis

* Identifies products generating the highest sales revenue
* Supports inventory and marketing decisions

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers for:

* Region
* Category
* Segment
* Order Date

These filters allow users to dynamically explore data and generate customized insights.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Technology category generated the highest sales.
* West region recorded the highest profit.
* Consumer segment contributed the largest share of revenue.
* Total sales exceeded **2.30 Million**.
* Profit margin reached approximately **12.47%**.
* A small number of products generated a significant portion of overall revenue.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase focus on high-performing product categories.
* Expand successful strategies implemented in profitable regions.
* Strengthen engagement with Consumer and Corporate customer segments.
* Improve profitability in lower-performing regions and categories.
* Continuously monitor top-selling products to maintain revenue growth.
